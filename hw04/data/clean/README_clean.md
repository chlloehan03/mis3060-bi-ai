## Transaction sign convention

All `amount` values are positive. Direction comes from `txn_type`, and depends on the perspective:

* **Cash-balance perspective** (cash moving in or out of the account's cash balance): inflow = Deposit, Sell, Dividend; outflow = Withdrawal, Buy, Advisory Fee. Buy/Sell/Dividend directions apply only here - a Buy or Sell moves value between cash and securities inside the account.
* **Net external flows** (money entering or leaving the client's account, e.g. for AUM change analysis): count only Deposit (+) and Withdrawal (-). Buy, Sell, Dividend and Advisory Fee are not external flows.

Neither perspective is documented by the source; this is the Part C interpretation of DQ0048. Apply the sign in a measure before netting. Exclude `flag_dividend_amount_miscalculated = 1` from any dividend or income total.

## Remaining limitations

* **Dividend income cannot be reported** - all 53,826 dividend amounts are miscalculated and the correct payouts are not in the data (Decision 1).
* **Holdings cannot be rebuilt from transactions** - use `fact_portfolio_snapshot` for positions/AUM (Decision 2).
* **Returns appear to include deposits/withdrawals** (not time-weighted); no methodology is documented. The -0.5/1.0 extreme-return thresholds are assumptions (Decision 3).
* **Fee revenue is not reconciled** - Advisory Fee transactions (~$263.7M) and `fact_fees` (~$204.7M) disagree; never add them together and always name the source table (Decision 4).
* **AUM = 1000 and large month-over-month jumps are flagged, not corrected**; fees on those balances may be wrong (Decision 5).
* **Fee rate cap (100 bps) and tier bands are inferred**, not an official schedule (Decision 6).
* **75 clients have risk_profile 'Unknown'** and default to benchmark 3; their alpha is unreliable (Decision 7).
* **ZIP/state mismatches affect 89% of clients**; do not use geography without a reference check (Decision 8).
* **Possible duplicate clients/securities** (shared names) were not merged (Decision 9).
* **7,350 snapshot client-months have no fee record**; these are reported in validation (Part D), not created or flagged here (Decision 11).
* **Departure is a proxy** (last snapshot), not a recorded date (Decision 13).
* **`dim_date.is_trading_day` behaves like 'weekday'** - 9 market holidays are marked as trading days and were left unchanged; trades on those holidays are not flagged (Decision 14).
* **Categories are observed, not verified** against official definitions (Decision 18).
* Numeric columns other than `return_ytd`/`active_alpha_ytd` keep their raw text (e.g. `426254.0`); they parse to the same numbers, but are not padded to a fixed number of decimals.

## Ambiguous decisions (interpretations made in Part C)

* Decision 2 says to flag pre-window rows as Informational; this was implemented as two flags (first oversell vs. later negative-position trades) to mirror the in-window split and DQ0056/DQ0057.
* Decision 7 says to 'flag these clients'; the flag is on `dim_client` only. Snapshots inherit it through `client_id` rather than getting a duplicated snapshot-level flag.
* Decision 14 says 'weekend-dated'; weekend = `dim_date.day_of_week` Saturday/Sunday, which is identical to `is_trading_day = 0` in this calendar (asserted).
* Decision 16 was applied to every value of both columns (not only the scientific-notation ones) so each column has one format. Three alpha values written `-0.0` become `-0.000000` (still zero; sign kept to avoid an undocumented change).
* Decision 5's AUM-jump flag sits on the later month of each jump; a client's first snapshot can never be flagged because it has no prior month.
* Decisions 4 and 11 call for reporting rather than row flags, so no flag columns were added for them.
* Boolean text (`True`/`False`) and source 0/1 flags were not recoded, because the decision log does not ask for it; cast them in the BI tool.
